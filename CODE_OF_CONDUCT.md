# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Laghu community respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior includes:

- Treating other people with respect and empathy
- Welcoming differing technical perspectives and experience levels
- Giving and receiving constructive, evidence-based feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and helping correct them
- Keeping discussions focused on the project and the work

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional project

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Laghu project spaces and when someone officially represents the project.

Maintainers may moderate contributions and interactions that violate these standards. Security vulnerabilities must not be reported as conduct concerns; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [admin@codevedas.com](mailto:admin@codevedas.com). Include the relevant context and links without sharing unrelated private information. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when appropriate.
