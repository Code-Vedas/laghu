# Why Laghu Exists

For years, `ngx_pagespeed` and its shared optimization library offered an
install-once way to rewrite web content at the server. Google archived the
project, leaving operators without releases, security maintenance, current
NGINX compatibility, or modern image and Core Web Vitals support.

Laghu exists to rebuild that operational experience on a maintainable
architecture. It is not a source port and does not expose legacy branding or
directives at runtime. The goal is a current, measurable, fail-open optimizer
whose heavy work cannot stall NGINX workers.
