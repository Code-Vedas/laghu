# mod-laghu RUM, Redis, and Instrumentation

Instrumentation requires an eligible complete HTML response, effective beacon settings, a CSP that permits the fixed same-origin script, and a report that passes origin, MIME, size, catalog, and rate-limit checks.

For Redis/Valkey, verify the main-server settings seen by every child, hiredis library loading, `rediss://` certificate chain and hostname, credentials, Lua permission, timeout, key prefix, and backend persistence.
Repeated wrong-type or contract errors indicate incompatible data under the configured namespace and should be diagnosed before purge.

Children may temporarily hold different memory views during backend loss.
The background synchronizer retains bounded pending deltas and converges through atomic idempotent batches after recovery.
Use template readiness and pending/synchronization metrics rather than raw key inspection as the normal operational interface.
